# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Added a four-step first-install journey setup with experience, program, preferences and a weekly-plan review. Existing users retain their setup.

- Added a separate Workout programs section in Settings.
- Added Body-part Split: Chest/Triceps (9), Back/Biceps (9), Legs (6), Shoulders (6), Cardio (6) and Arms (6). Friday/Saturday order is selectable.
- Added General Health: three full-body strength sessions plus optional movement days, with walking or cycling guidance.
- Included alternatives, level-specific workload, warm-up/rest/recovery guidance and four-week consistency in both new programs.
- Preserved started weeks, saved substitutions and history when changing programs; future untouched weeks use the selection.
- Kept automatic level progression within the chosen program.
- Updated Android versionCode to 2 and app/package version to 1.0.1.

## 1.0.0

Initial public release with training levels, local progress, exercise alternatives, missed-day handling and reminders.
