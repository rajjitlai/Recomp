## Exercise image credits

Recomp includes 186 exercise images. The complete image-by-image attribution index is [`image_sources.csv`](./image_sources.csv); it maps every local asset to its source platform, listed license or terms, and direct source URL.

- **180 images** are from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), which is distributed under [The Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **6 images** are from Wikimedia Commons. Their direct source URLs and the license/terms recorded for each image are listed in the attribution index. Check the individual source page for the creator and exact license details before reusing those images outside this project.

The image files are stored under [`assets/exercises/`](./assets/exercises/). The attribution index records paths relative to that directory.
